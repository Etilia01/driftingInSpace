A renpy visual novel set on a spaceship that got quarantined for no apparent reason. So its up to you to find out why! (or maybe not...?)
There are curently 5 available endings: 1 bad, 1 good, 3 neutral!

I made EVERYTHING (except the engine ofc, thats just renpy) myself: art, music, script EVERYTHING no ai used whatsoever! (thats why its kind of meh- at least the music. My art is great!)
I was kind of under a lot of time pressure making this so some dialogue may feel a bit rushed... 

This was my first time making custom animations and a behind-the-scenes point system (using variables) for choices/personality traits that changes the dialogue in renpy, and i learned a lot! I also struggled quite a bit with the pronoun system, even tho i had attempted something similar previously... but with (a lot of) tutorials i figured that one out too!
this whole project took me more than 30 hours btw T-T But it was totally worth it! This is only the beta tho... more routes and features coming soon :) !
